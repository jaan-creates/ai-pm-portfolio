# Product Manager, Developer Platform (Public API)
**Company:** Solstice Commerce Cloud · **Location:** Bengaluru (hybrid) · **Team:** Developer Platform

## About the role
Solstice's public API is our product for thousands of external developers who build commerce integrations on top of us. We're hiring a Platform PM to own the developer-facing API surface: how it's versioned, how it's documented, how it deprecates, and how it feels to build against. Your users are engineers, and your job is to make their integration work predictable and pleasant — while keeping a large, backward-compatible surface from collapsing under its own history.

## What you'll do
- Own the public API roadmap: what we expose, how we version it, and how we sunset it without breaking partners.
- Design and enforce a deprecation policy — timelines, migration guides, and developer comms — that partners can plan around.
- Run developer discovery: read support tickets and forum threads, interview integrators, and turn their friction into roadmap.
- Make hard backward-compatibility calls (webhooks, pagination, rate-limit behaviour) and own the consequences.
- Partner with DevRel and docs so the reference, guides, and changelog stay trustworthy.

## Requirements (must have)
- 4+ years in product, with demonstrated ownership of a developer-facing or API product; **required**.
- Evidence of real versioning or deprecation decisions you made and their outcome; **required**.
- Ability to run discovery with a developer audience (not end-consumers); **required**.
- Enough technical fluency to reason about API design tradeoffs — REST/gRPC, webhooks, idempotency, rate limiting — and defend a call to engineers; **required**.

## Nice to have
- Experience with an event-driven or streaming integration surface.
- Prior DevRel or engineering background.
- Commerce or payments domain exposure.

## How we work
We care about *decisions*, not tool vocabulary. Anyone can list "gRPC, Kafka, OpenAPI" on a resume; we want the person who chose a versioning scheme, argued a deprecation window with a reluctant partner, and can tell you what breaking change they refused to ship and why. Depth over surface.

## Who you'll work with
You'll partner daily with a DevRel lead, a docs writer, and two platform engineering teams, and you'll chair the monthly partner council where our largest integrators tell us what our API decisions cost them. The interview loop includes a written exercise: given a real backward-compatibility dilemma from our changelog, write the deprecation plan you'd defend to that council.
