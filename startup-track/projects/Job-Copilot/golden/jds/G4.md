# Product Manager, ML Risk Products
**Company:** Northwind Risk Systems · **Location:** Remote (global; async-first) · **Team:** Applied ML

## About the role
Northwind builds ML-powered risk products — models that score transactions for fraud and abuse in real time. We're hiring a PM who has actually shipped machine-learning products and lived with their consequences: the false positives that block good customers, the false negatives that cost money, and the eval discipline that keeps the tradeoff honest. This is not a "GenAI strategy" role. It is a role for someone who can sit with data science, argue a precision/recall operating point, and own what happens when the model is wrong.

## What you'll do
- Own an ML risk product end to end: problem framing, labelling strategy, eval design, launch, and the post-launch failure-mode loop.
- Make and defend the precision/recall (or equivalent) operating-point decision with data science, and translate it into a business tradeoff leadership understands.
- Design offline and online evaluation: metrics, holdouts, drift monitoring, and the guardrails that catch a model degrading in production.
- Own incident response when the model misbehaves — root-cause, mitigate, and feed the fix back into training data.
- Build the data flywheel: how feedback and labels improve the next model version.

## Requirements (must have)
- 4+ years in product, including at least one ML/AI product you shipped and owned in production; **required**.
- Demonstrated ownership of an evaluation design — named metrics, a precision/recall or threshold decision you made; **required**.
- Experience handling an ML failure mode or model incident in production; **required**.
- Fluency talking to data scientists about model behaviour, data quality, and drift; **required**.

## Nice to have
- Risk, fraud, trust & safety, or fintech domain experience.
- Familiarity with real-time/streaming inference constraints.

## How we work
We draw a hard line between *AI-adjacent* and *AI-product*. Building a GPT wrapper, running prompt-engineering workshops, or authoring an "AI strategy" deck is not the same as shipping a model, choosing its operating point, and owning its failures. We're hiring for the latter. Show us the eval you designed and the tradeoff you defended.
