# Technical Product Manager — Payments Platform
**Company:** Kestrel Financial Technologies · **Location:** Bengaluru (hybrid, 3 days in office) · **Team:** Payments Platform

## About the role
Kestrel's Payments Platform powers settlement, reconciliation, and ledger services for 40+ internal product teams. As Technical Product Manager you are the connective tissue that keeps a large, interdependent release train moving. You will sit at the centre of engineering, QA, compliance, and the consuming product teams, and your core job is to make sure the right things ship, in the right order, without breaking the teams downstream of us. This is a delivery-intensive role on a platform where a missed dependency can stall a dozen teams.

## What you'll do
- Own the quarterly release calendar for the platform: sequence features across 5 engineering squads, publish the plan, and keep it current as scope moves.
- Run the platform's agile ceremonies — sprint planning, backlog refinement, daily stand-ups, retrospectives — across squads and drive them to crisp outcomes.
- Map cross-team dependencies ahead of each release, chase the blockers, and escalate risk early with a clear mitigation path.
- Produce weekly status reporting for platform leadership and consuming teams: what shipped, what slipped, what's at risk, and why.
- Coordinate release readiness: cutover plans, rollback criteria, compliance sign-offs, and go/no-go reviews.
- Keep the platform's JIRA hygiene, release notes, and runbooks accurate and discoverable.

## Requirements (must have)
- 6+ years managing delivery of software in a multi-team environment; **required**.
- Demonstrated ownership of a release process across several engineering teams; **required**.
- Fluency in agile delivery mechanics (ceremonies, dependency mapping, risk/RAID logs); **required**.
- Strong written status communication to technical and leadership audiences; **required**.
- Comfort with the technical detail of platform/API services (you don't build, but you must reason about integration order); **required**.

## Nice to have
- Exposure to product discovery or roadmap definition.
- Background in payments, ledgers, or regulated financial systems.
- A technical degree or prior engineering experience.

## How we work
Delivery predictability is the product here. Consuming teams trust us when releases land when we said they would. We measure ourselves on schedule adherence, dependency lead time, and the number of downstream teams unblocked per release. If you take satisfaction in a complex program running on time, you'll do well.
