# Lead Product Manager — Seller Experience
**Company:** Meridian Commerce · **Location:** Bengaluru (hybrid) · **Team:** Seller Experience

## About the role
Meridian's Seller Experience area spans onboarding, catalogue, pricing, and seller analytics — four squads, four PMs, one shared north star. We're hiring a Lead PM to own the *strategy* for this whole area: not to run a feature, but to set the direction that four teams execute, decide what we stop doing, and grow the PMs underneath you. This is an area-level leadership role. You will be measured on the coherence and outcomes of the area, not the output of any single squad.

## What you'll do
- Own and author the Seller Experience area strategy; get it adopted across four squads and defended to leadership.
- Make area-level prioritisation tradeoffs — including which bets to kill and which teams to reallocate.
- Influence a multi-team roadmap without owning every team directly; align four PMs on a shared plan.
- Mentor and grow 2–4 PMs and APMs; own their development, not just their deliverables.
- Set the area's success metrics and be accountable for them at the leadership table.

## Requirements (must have)
- 7+ years in product, with evidence of owning **strategy for a product area** (not a single feature); **required**.
- Demonstrated influence across multiple teams you did not directly manage; **required**.
- Track record of mentoring or growing other PMs; **required**.
- Accountability for area-level outcomes and the prioritisation tradeoffs behind them; **required**.

## Nice to have
- Marketplace or seller-side commerce experience.
- Experience authoring a strategy that was adopted org-wide.

## How we work
Level here is measured by evidenced scope, not title. A "Senior PM" who has owned an area strategy and grown two PMs is a Lead to us; a "Lead" whose evidence is six years of excellent single-feature work is not, however good that work was. Tell us the strategy you authored, who adopted it, and which PM you grew.

## Scope and stakeholders
The Seller Experience area sits at the centre of the marketplace: your strategy affects the seller-facing roadmap for roughly a third of the company's engineering headcount, and you'll defend it quarterly to the VP of Product and the head of Seller Operations. The final interview stage is a strategy review — you'll present a 30-60-90 view of how you'd take an unfamiliar product area from four disconnected roadmaps to one coherent bet.
