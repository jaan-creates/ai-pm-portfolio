# Senior Product Manager — Enterprise Platform
**Company:** Ferrocast Systems (B2B SaaS) · **Location:** Remote (global; overlap with US Eastern) · **Team:** Enterprise Product

## About the role
Ferrocast sells a compliance-and-audit platform to large regulated enterprises — banks, insurers, hospital systems. Deals are big, slow, and committee-driven: a typical sales cycle runs six months and passes through procurement, security review, and a legal redline before anyone signs. Product decisions here live or die by whether they survive that gauntlet. We're hiring a Senior PM who has actually shipped enterprise B2B products into long, complex buying processes and knows how different that is from a self-serve motion.

## What you'll do
- Own a core area of the enterprise platform: roadmap, discovery with enterprise buyers and users, and delivery.
- Partner with sales and solutions engineering on the six-month deal cycle: security questionnaires, procurement requirements, and proof-of-concept criteria that turn into product work.
- Translate the demands of regulated enterprise buyers (audit trails, SSO/SCIM, data residency, admin controls) into a roadmap.
- Balance the needs of the end user with the very different needs of the economic buyer and the security reviewer.

## Requirements (must have)
- **Experience shipping enterprise B2B products into long, complex sales cycles (6 months or more) — required.** This is the non-negotiable requirement for this role; a self-serve or purely product-led-growth background does not substitute.
- 6+ years in product; **required**.
- Discovery skill with enterprise buyers and users (distinct from consumer/self-serve users); **required**.

## Nice to have
- Compliance, audit, or regulated-industry domain exposure.
- Familiarity with enterprise buying artifacts: security questionnaires, procurement, MSAs.

## How we work
We are explicit about the must-have because we've been burned: a brilliant product-led-growth PM with no enterprise-cycle experience will optimise for the wrong buyer and stall in procurement. If you haven't shipped into a six-month enterprise cycle, this specific role isn't the fit — however strong your other product work is. If you have, tell us the deal you helped close and the product decision that got it through security review.

## Who you'll work with
You'll partner with two solutions engineers, an enterprise sales team, and a security-and-compliance lead who owns the questionnaire our buyers send us. Expect to spend real time with design partners inside regulated enterprises, not in a usability lab. The interview loop centres on one conversation: walk us through an enterprise deal where a product decision you owned was the thing that moved it forward — or the thing that stalled it.
