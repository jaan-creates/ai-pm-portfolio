# Growth Product Manager — Consumer Subscription
**Company:** Tidewater Media · **Location:** Remote (global; overlap with US Pacific hours) · **Team:** Growth

## About the role
Tidewater is a consumer subscription app (audio + long-form) with millions of monthly actives and a healthy but plateauing funnel. We're hiring a Growth PM to own activation and early retention: the stretch of the journey between a new signup and a habituated, paying subscriber. You will run a high-tempo experimentation program and be accountable for a specific set of funnel metrics — not for "growth" in the abstract, but for numbers you move and can defend.

## What you'll do
- Own activation and week-4 retention as your primary metrics; set the targets and report against them.
- Run 2–4 experiments per sprint across onboarding, paywall, and habit loops; maintain the experiment backlog and kill losers fast.
- Define and defend guardrail metrics so a win on activation doesn't quietly cost us refunds or churn.
- Partner with data science on experiment design, power analysis, and readout discipline.
- Translate wins into durable product changes, and write the honest post-mortem when an experiment fails.

## Requirements (must have)
- 4+ years in product, with at least 2 years owning a funnel or lifecycle metric end to end; **required**.
- Demonstrated ownership of a named metric you moved, with the mechanism and magnitude; **required**.
- Hands-on experimentation experience: hypotheses, variants, power, readouts — you have personally run and decided from A/B tests; **required**.
- Fluency with product analytics (SQL or an equivalent) to interrogate your own funnels; **required**.

## Nice to have
- Consumer subscription or media experience.
- Familiarity with paywall and pricing experimentation.
- Exposure to retention/lifecycle messaging.

## How we work
We are allergic to vanity metrics. "Engagement went up" is not a result here; "activation moved 12%→18% across nine experiments, guardrail on refund rate held" is. We reward owned, defensible, mechanism-level impact over big round numbers you happened to be near. Bring receipts.

## Team and process
You'll sit inside a small growth pod — one PM, two engineers, a designer, and a shared data scientist — and you'll own the experiment backlog for that pod outright. We ship behind flags, review readouts every Thursday, and expect a written decision (ship / iterate / kill) on every experiment within a day of the readout. The interview process is a portfolio conversation about two experiments you ran end to end, followed by a working session on an activation problem from our own funnel.
