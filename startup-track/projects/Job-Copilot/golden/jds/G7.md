# Product Manager / Product Owner — Digital Services
**Company:** Tellwright Digital (IT Services) · **Location:** Bengaluru / Hyderabad (hybrid) · **Team:** Client Delivery

## About the role
Tellwright builds digital products for enterprise clients in an agile delivery model. This is a hybrid PM/PO role: you own the product outcomes for a client engagement while also playing the Product Owner inside the delivery squad. We work in a ceremony-heavy environment because our clients expect it — but we are hiring for the person who drives *outcomes* through that process, not the person who merely operates it flawlessly. Certifications are common here and welcome; they are table stakes, not evidence.

## What you'll do
- Own product outcomes for one or more client engagements: understand the client's real problem, shape the roadmap, and be accountable for the result, not just the delivery.
- Play Product Owner in the squad: groom the backlog, write stories, and keep the team unblocked — while never losing the thread of *why* we're building it.
- Translate client goals into a prioritised product plan and defend the tradeoffs.
- Run discovery with client stakeholders and end users; bring evidence, not opinions, to prioritisation.
- Show measurable outcomes at the end of an engagement, not just a shipped backlog.

## Requirements (must have)
- 5+ years in product/PO roles, with **evidence of an outcome you drove** (a client metric moved, a problem measurably solved) — not only ceremonies run; **required**.
- Ability to own the "why" and the roadmap, not just the backlog; **required**.
- Comfort in an agile, ceremony-heavy delivery environment; **required**.
- Stakeholder discovery and prioritisation skill; **required**.

## Nice to have
- Agile certifications (CSPO, PSM, SAFe, PMP) — welcome but not a substitute for outcome evidence.
- Enterprise / client-services experience.

## How we work
We have plenty of certified people who can run a flawless ceremony and show you a tidy burndown. What's rarer, and what we're paying for, is the PO who can point to a client outcome they changed. If your strongest signal is your certifications, this isn't the role. If you can tell us the outcome you drove and how, it is.
