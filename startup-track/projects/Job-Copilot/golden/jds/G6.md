# Product Manager, Digital Lending
**Company:** Rupaya Financial Services · **Location:** Bengaluru (hybrid) · **Team:** Lending

## About the role
Rupaya is an RBI-regulated NBFC building digital lending products for underserved borrowers. We're hiring a Domain PM for our lending stack: origination, underwriting, disbursal, and collections. This is a role where domain is not a nice-to-have — it is the job. You will make product decisions inside a live regulatory perimeter (RBI Digital Lending Guidelines, fair-practices code, data-localisation, KYC/AML), and a decision that ignores the regulation is not a clever shortcut, it's a compliance incident. We need someone who has built lending or comparable regulated-financial products and knows where the landmines are.

## What you'll do
- Own product decisions across the lending lifecycle within the RBI regulatory perimeter.
- Translate RBI Digital Lending Guidelines and fair-practices requirements into product requirements engineering can build.
- Partner with risk, credit, and compliance on underwriting policy, disclosure flows (KFS), and collections conduct rules.
- Own domain metrics that matter for a lender: approval rate vs. delinquency, cost of acquisition vs. lifetime value, and regulatory-disclosure completion.
- Navigate audits and regulatory reviews with the product evidence they require.

## Requirements (must have)
- 5+ years in product, with **direct experience building a regulated financial product** (lending, payments, insurance, or comparable); **required**.
- Demonstrated work inside a named compliance regime (RBI, or an equivalent regulator) — not just "worked at a fintech"; **required**.
- Fluency with lending or credit domain metrics and the risk tradeoffs behind them; **required**.
- Experience partnering with compliance/risk as a first-class product constraint; **required**.

## Nice to have
- Direct RBI Digital Lending Guidelines experience.
- Collections or underwriting product background.

## How we work
A brilliant generalist PM with no regulated-domain scars will get hurt here, and so will we. We would rather have a very good PM who has actually shipped inside a regulator's perimeter than a spectacular one who hasn't. Domain evidence is the gate; PM craft is assumed.

## About Rupaya
We serve borrowers that traditional lenders overlook, and we hold ourselves to the RBI's conduct standards as a matter of survival, not compliance theatre — a single fair-practices misstep can cost us our licence. You'll work alongside a credit-risk team, a compliance officer, and an in-house counsel who reviews every borrower-facing flow. The interview process includes a scenario walk-through: we'll hand you a lending-flow decision with a regulatory wrinkle and ask how you'd resolve it.
